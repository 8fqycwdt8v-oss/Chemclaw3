Running the biaryl coupling at 100 °C in THF/water raises LCAP conversion and *lowers* isolated
yield: the boronic acid protodeboronates to benzene faster than it transmetalates.

Observed three times before it was recognised, because the assay looked better each time.

[[contradicts:bo-suzuki-next]] to the extent that the model proposes higher temperature; it has not
seen this failure because it was fitted on assay yield.
