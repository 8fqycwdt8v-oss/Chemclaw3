Fischer esterification of acetic acid with ethanol, H2SO4 catalytic, reflux 6 h.

- equilibrium-limited: 67% conversion at equilibrium with no water removal
- with a Dean-Stark trap: 94%

The instructive point is that the yield is a statement about the *setup*, not the chemistry.
Compare [[analogue-of:rxn-aspirin-acetylation]], where the anhydride makes the step irreversible
and the equilibrium question does not arise.
