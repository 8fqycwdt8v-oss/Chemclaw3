**Q:** Is 1.5 mol% Pd really necessary, or can we halve it?

**A (confirmed):** At 0.75 mol% the coupling reaches ~50% conversion in 12 h and stalls. It is not
a rate effect that longer time fixes — the catalyst is dying. Halving the loading is viable only
together with better degassing; see [[cites:playbook-degassing]].
