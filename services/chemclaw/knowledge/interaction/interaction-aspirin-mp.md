**Q:** Our aspirin melts at 128-131 °C, literature says 135 °C. Is the batch bad?

**A (confirmed):** A depressed and broadened range is residual solvent or salicylic acid, not a
different compound. Recrystallise from ethanol/water with a slow ramp and re-check; see
[[cites:playbook-recrystallisation-purity]].
