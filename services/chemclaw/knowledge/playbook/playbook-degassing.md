## Degas properly for Pd(0), or accept a bimodal yield distribution

Distilled from [[cites:opt-suzuki-conditions]].

Pd(0) is oxidised by dissolved oxygen and the resulting black precipitate is catalytically dead.
The signature is a *bimodal* outcome — nominally identical runs give 70%+ or under 20%, rarely
anything between — because it depends on whether the catalyst survived its first minutes.

Three freeze-pump-thaw cycles, or sparging for 20 minutes per 100 mL. Sparging for two minutes is
the same as not degassing.

Replaces [[supersedes:playbook-degassing-old]], which recommended exactly that.
