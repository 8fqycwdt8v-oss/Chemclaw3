Repeated runs of the biaryl coupling under varied temperature, Pd loading and base.

- transformation: [[rxn-suzuki-biaryl]]
- best found: 1.5 mol% Pd, K2CO3, 80 °C — 76% isolated
- 100 °C gave 79% by LCAP and 68% isolated, the difference being protodeboronation

The gap between assay and isolated yield is the finding: optimising on the wrong readout would have
selected the hotter, worse condition. See [[failure-aqueous-protodeboronation]].
