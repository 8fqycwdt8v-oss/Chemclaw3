Anhydrous zinc bromide. The salt the arylzinc bromide in this programme carries as its counter-ion,
and the additive used to keep a zinc-poor insertion from stalling part way. Dried at 150 °C under
vacuum before use; wet zinc bromide is as bad as wet zinc chloride.
