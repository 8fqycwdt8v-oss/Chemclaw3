4-Methoxyphenylboronic acid, the electron-rich boronic acid screened beside phenylboronic acid as
the transmetalating partner in the Suzuki coupling. Assay by qNMR before use: commercial material
carries a variable boroxine fraction, and the boronic acid equivalents that matter are the ones
actually in the flask.
