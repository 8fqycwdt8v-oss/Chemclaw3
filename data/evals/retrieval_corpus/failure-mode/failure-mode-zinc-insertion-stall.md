Zinc insertion stalls when the zinc dust is not activated. The batch shows an induction period that
never ends: no exotherm, no consumption of the aryl bromide, and a zinc surface that stays grey.
Activation with 1,2-dibromoethane and TMSCl restarts it; adding more zinc does not.
