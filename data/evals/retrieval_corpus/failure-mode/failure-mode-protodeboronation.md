Protodeboronation of the boronic acid under hot aqueous base. The boronic acid is consumed without
producing product, so the coupling looks slow when it is actually starved. Diagnostic: benzene in
the headspace and a boronic acid assay that falls faster than product forms. Mitigation is a lower
base strength, a slower boronic acid charge, or the pinacol boronate.
