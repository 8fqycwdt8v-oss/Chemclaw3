Oxygen leaking into the coupling oxidises Pd(0) and drives homocoupling of the boronic acid to
biphenyl. The tell is that the impurity tracks headspace oxygen rather than temperature. Degassing
is the control; see [[playbook-degassing-air-sensitive]].
