Preparing an arylzinc for a Negishi coupling. Activate the zinc dust first, hold the insertion under
a nitrogen sweep, titrate the organozinc before transfer, and never filter a warm zinc slurry in
air. The zinc handling, not the coupling, is what makes this route expensive.
