Degassing for air-sensitive catalysis. Three sparge volumes of nitrogen through the subsurface dip
tube, then a vacuum-nitrogen cycle on the headspace; measure the dissolved oxygen rather than
counting cycles. Every low-loading coupling in this programme depends on this being done properly.
