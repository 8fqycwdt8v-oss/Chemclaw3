General palladium cross-coupling playbook: ligand selection, base and solvent choices, and degassing
to protect the catalyst. Covers the cross-coupling families this programme uses — Suzuki, Negishi,
Heck and Sonogashira — and the order in which to change a variable when a cross-coupling
underperforms.
