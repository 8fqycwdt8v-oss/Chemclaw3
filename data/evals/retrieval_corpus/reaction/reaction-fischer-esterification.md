Fischer esterification of acetic acid with ethanol, concentrated sulfuric acid catalyst, reflux to
drive the equilibrium toward the ester. Held at reflux for eight hours in a fourfold excess of
ethanol, which is what drives it: a Dean–Stark trap separates nothing here, because ethanol boils
below water and is miscible with it.
