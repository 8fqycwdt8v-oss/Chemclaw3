Confirmed with the process chemist: K3PO4 over K2CO3 for this coupling. The carbonate is cheaper and
gives the same conversion at 80 °C, which is reflux in 2-MeTHF, but the phosphate holds the boronic
acid intact for the whole hold, which is what the campaign was actually optimising.
