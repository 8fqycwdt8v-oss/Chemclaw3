Stability of the boronic acid under the coupling conditions. The free boronic acid loses about 4%
per hour at 80 °C in aqueous base; the pinacol boronate is stable but transmetalates slowly.
Protodeboronation is the dominant loss path and is written up in [[failure-mode-protodeboronation]].
