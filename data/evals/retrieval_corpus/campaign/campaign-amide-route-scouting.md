Route scouting for the amide bond in the second step. Carbodiimide, uronium and acyl-imidazole
activations were compared on 2 g scale; the carbodiimide route was carried forward because its
by-product is removed in the aqueous wash. Coupling of the acid with the amine was clean in every
case, so the choice was decided by work-up rather than by conversion.
