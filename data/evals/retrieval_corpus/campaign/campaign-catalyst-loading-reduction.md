Reducing the palladium loading from 2 mol% to 0.3 mol% across the Suzuki coupling step. The catalyst
survives the reduction only with rigorous degassing; see [[playbook-degassing-air-sensitive]].
Residual palladium in the isolated solid tracks the loading almost linearly, which is what makes the
reduction worth the extra handling.
