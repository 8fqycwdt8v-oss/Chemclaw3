Swapping THF for 2-MeTHF across the Suzuki coupling and the work-up. 2-MeTHF phase-separates from
water without an emulsion and boils higher, so the reaction can be run at reflux rather than in a
sealed vessel. Yield was within experimental error of the THF process.
