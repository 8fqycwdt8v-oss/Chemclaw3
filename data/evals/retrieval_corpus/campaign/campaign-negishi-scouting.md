Negishi coupling scouted as an alternative to the Suzuki step. The organozinc is made in situ from
the aryl bromide and zinc dust; the zinc insertion is the step that decides whether the campaign is
viable at all. Coupling yields matched the Suzuki route on 5 g, but the zinc handling is the reason
it was not carried forward. See [[playbook-organozinc-preparation]].
