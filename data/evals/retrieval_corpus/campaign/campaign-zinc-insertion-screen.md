Screen of zinc activation methods for the insertion: zinc dust with 1,2-dibromoethane and TMSCl,
Rieke zinc, and commercial zinc-copper couple. Activation decides the induction period; unactivated
zinc dust stalls entirely, which is recorded in [[failure-mode-zinc-insertion-stall]].
