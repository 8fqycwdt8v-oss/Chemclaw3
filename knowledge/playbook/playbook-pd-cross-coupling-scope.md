## When a palladium coupling stalls, change the ligand before the temperature

Distilled from [[cites:campaign-biaryl-scope]] and [[cites:opt-suzuki-conditions]].

Oxidative addition into an electron-rich aryl halide is the slow step, and it responds to the
ligand's steric bulk far more than to temperature. Raising the temperature accelerates the
competing decomposition of the boronic acid at least as much as it accelerates the coupling — which
is exactly what [[cites:failure-aqueous-protodeboronation]] records happening.

Order to try: bulkier biaryl phosphine → higher L:Pd → degas harder → only then more heat.
