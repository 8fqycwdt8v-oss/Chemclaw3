## Use HOBt (or an equivalent) with EDC on anything but the simplest substrate

Distilled from [[cites:campaign-amide-additive]].

EDC alone forms an O-acylisourea that rearranges irreversibly to the unreactive N-acylurea. The
additive intercepts it as an active ester faster than the rearrangement happens. The cost is one
cheap reagent; the benefit measured here was ~25 points.

Does not apply to activated acids or to coupling reagents that form the active ester directly
(HATU, T3P).
