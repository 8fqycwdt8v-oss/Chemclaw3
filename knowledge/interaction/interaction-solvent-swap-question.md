**Q:** Can we run the EDC coupling in 2-MeTHF instead of DMF?

**A (confirmed):** Yes, at a cost of about 5 points of yield. [[cites:opt-amide-solvent]] measured
79% in MeCN and 76% in 2-MeTHF against 81% in DMF, on the same substrate. 2-MeTHF is the better
choice for anything going to scale — it is bio-derived, it is not a reprotoxin, and it separates
from water, which removes the DMF work-up entirely.
