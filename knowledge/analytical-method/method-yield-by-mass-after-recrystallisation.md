## Isolated yield by mass, after recrystallisation from ethanol/water

Dry the recrystallised solid to constant mass under vacuum at ambient temperature, weigh, and
report against the theoretical mass from the limiting reagent.

**What this method measures, and what it does not.** It measures the mass of solid recovered after
one recrystallisation. It says nothing about the purity of that solid: a yield reported this way
carries whatever the recrystallisation rejected and whatever it did not, and two runs quoted this
way are comparable only if both were recrystallised the same way. Where a purity figure is wanted
beside the yield, it has to come from a method that measures purity —
[[playbook-recrystallisation-purity]] is the rule about saying which was used.

Seed content: the procedure is ordinary and the note exists so the `analytical-method` type and the
`measured-by` edge have a real instance to be exercised against, per `knowledge/README.md`.
