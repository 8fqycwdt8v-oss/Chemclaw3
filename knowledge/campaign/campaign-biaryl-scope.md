Six couplings exploring aryl-halide scope at fixed catalyst and base, run to answer one question:
where does this system stop working?

- [[rxn-suzuki-biaryl]]
- [[rxn-buchwald-amination]]

Finding: electron-rich bromides couple at 80 °C in 12 h; electron-rich *chlorides* do not couple
at all under these conditions. That boundary, not the yields, is the transferable result.
