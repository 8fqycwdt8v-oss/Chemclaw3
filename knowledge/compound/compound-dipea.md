N,N-diisopropylethylamine. A hindered tertiary amine base — basic enough to deprotonate a
carboxylic acid, too hindered to compete as a nucleophile, which is what makes it the default in
amide couplings.

- also written: DIPEA, Hunig's base, N,N-diisopropylethylamine

The base in [[reagent-in:rxn-amide-edc]] and the amine catalyst in
[[catalyzes:rxn-aspirin-acetylation]].
